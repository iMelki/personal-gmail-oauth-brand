Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

Milky Local Agent Gmail is a private desktop automation operated only by its owner. It is not offered to the public.

Data accessed

After the operator grants access, the app requests the Google OAuth scopes gmail.readonly, openid, userinfo.email, and userinfo.profile. That allows it to verify the signed-in mailbox and to read message metadata and bodies needed for local monitoring. It does not request send, delete, or settings-modification scopes.

How data is used

Mail is processed only on the operator's local computer to classify, log, and surface items for human review. Data is not sold, advertised against, or shared with third parties. Encrypted Google tokens are stored in the operator's OS user profile and keyring.

Retention

Local logs and state remain on the operator's machine until deleted. Google account access can be revoked at any time in Google Account security settings under third-party access.

Contact

Use the support email shown on the Google OAuth consent screen for this app. Do not use this page as a contact directory.
